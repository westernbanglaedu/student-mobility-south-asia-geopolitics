Indian Student Visa Guide for Bangladeshi Students

Complete visa requirements, application process, processing times, and practical guidance for Bangladeshi students seeking to study in India.

Understanding Indian Student Visas

Bangladeshi students planning to study in India require a specific Student Visa (S-Visa). Unlike tourist or business visas, the student visa is linked to your educational institution and comes with distinct conditions regarding duration, work rights, and extension requirements.

Visa Types for Indian Education

Student Visa (S-Visa) Mandatory

Required for all Bangladeshi students enrolled in recognized Indian educational institutions. Valid for the duration of your course plus additional time for registration processes.

Entry Visa (X-Visa) Post-Study

May be granted for short-term academic programs under 180 days. Check current regulations for specific program eligibility.

Medical Visa Specialized

For students pursuing medical studies where the program includes clinical practice. Requires additional documentation from medical institutions.

Research Visa Scholars

For doctoral candidates and research scholars working on approved research projects at Indian universities or research institutions.

Application Requirements

Essential Documents Checklist

  • Valid Passport: Minimum 6 months validity from intended travel date, with at least two blank pages
  • Visa Application Form: Completed online application with photograph upload
  • Admission Letter: Official acceptance letter from the Indian educational institution
  • Proof of Financial Capacity: Bank statements (last 6 months), fixed deposit certificates, or sponsor's affidavit with supporting documents
  • Educational Certificates: SSC, HSC, and any other relevant academic credentials with translations if needed
  • Passport-Size Photographs: Recent photos meeting ICAO standards (2"x2", white background)
  • Proof of Accommodation: University dormitory confirmation or rental agreement
  • Travel Insurance: Valid for the duration of initial stay
  • Visa Fee Receipt: Payment confirmation for visa application fee

Application Process

  1. Secure Admission First

    Visa applications require an official admission letter. Apply to Indian institutions first, receive acceptance, and obtain the official confirmation before initiating visa proceedings.

  2. Complete Online Application

    Submit the visa application form through the Indian Visa Application Center (IVAC) website. Upload required documents and photograph. Print the completed application form with barcode.

  3. Gather Supporting Documents

    Compile all required documents including original certificates, financial proof, passport, photographs, and admission letter. Ensure all documents are in order before visiting the application center.

  4. Visit IVAC or High Commission

    Submit your application at the Indian Visa Application Center in Dhaka or the High Commission of India. Pay the applicable visa fee and biometric enrollment may be required.

  5. Track Application Status

    Monitor your application status online using the tracking number provided. Processing typically takes 5–15 working days depending on application volume and individual case complexity.

  6. Collect Passport with Visa

    Once approved, collect your passport with the stamped visa from the application center. Verify all details including name, passport number, visa validity dates, and visa category.

Important Conditions

Student Visa Restrictions

Indian student visas come with specific conditions that all students must follow:

What You Cannot Do

  • Engage in any form of employment or paid work
  • Change institution or course without proper visa amendment
  • Stay beyond visa validity without extension
  • Apply for permanent residence through student visa pathways

Extension Requirements

Student visas can be extended within India through the Foreigners Regional Registration Office (FRRO). Extensions require:

  • Valid passport with remaining validity
  • Continued enrollment verification from institution
  • Academic progress reports
  • Financial proof for extended stay period

Strategic Timing

Begin visa applications only after receiving firm admission acceptance. Applying too early can create complications if admission falls through. Conversely, applying too late risks missing academic intake deadlines. Target submission 3–4 months before program start date.

Practical Tips

Application Center Navigation

The Indian Visa Application Center in Dhaka handles student visa applications. Arrive early during peak seasons (June–August for fall intake, November–December for spring intake). Online appointment booking can reduce wait times.

Document Preparation

Keep both original documents and certified copies. Some documents may require attestation by the Bangladesh Foreign Ministry or Chamber of Commerce. Start this process at least 4 weeks before your intended visa submission date.

Financial Documentation

Maintain consistent account balances rather than large, sudden deposits. Bank statements should clearly show regular income or savings patterns. If using a sponsor, ensure the sponsor affidavit follows the required format and includes their financial documents.
